# Needs a test build (make TEST=1), and the simulator (its e-paper panel):
# ui.epaper (docs/EPAPER.md) end to end, the real driver (src/runtime/
# epd.cpp) talking to EPDPages' SSD1680 model over the simulated SPI. The
# test card's gapkeep.txt configures a WeAct 2.9" B/W/R; panel "1" is
# plugged in at boot. tests/apps/epapertest writes codex32-like shares;
# tools/run_sim_tests.sh then checks the panels' pictures
# (tests/epaper/check_panel.py: the QR codes decode to the shares, the red
# title is red, panel 1 kept its share while panel 2 was written).
#
# SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later
expect Keyboard attached 60
expect epaper: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R \(128x296, red\) on SPI0 CE0 \(GPIO 8\), DC GPIO 25, RST GPIO 17, BUSY GPIO 24, 4000 kHz
expect epaper-sim: panel 1 \(WeAct 2.9" B/W/R\) plugged in
expect launcher ready 60
# a refresh holds BUSY 1.5 s here (18 s on the real panel)
epaper refresh-ms 1500
launch epapertest
expect epapertest ready
expect epapertest info: \{"model":"WeAct 2.9\\" B/W/R","width":296,"height":128,"colors":\["black","white","red"\]\}
expect epapertest open: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R, 296x128, black white red
expect epapertest: ok open twice
expect epapertest: ok unknown option
expect epapertest: ok bad orientation
expect epapertest: ok a Container
reject epapertest: FAIL
# opening touches no panel: nothing written yet
reject epaper: .*refresh

# share 1 on panel 1: reset, init, both planes, the waveform, deep sleep
mark
key 1
expect epaper: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R: refresh: reset
expect epaper-sim: panel 1: refresh 1 \(full\), BUSY for 1500 ms
expect epapertest progress: the waveform runs 10
expect epaper: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R: refresh: done in \d+ ms \(BUSY high \d+ us after 0x20\), asleep 10
expect epapertest wrote 1
# (the driver never sent a byte to a sleeping panel)
reject panel .*: bytes sent in deep sleep
shot 01-written
epaper shot 01-panel1

# unplugged: a clear error, and no refresh
epaper unplug
mark
key 2
expect epapertest failed 2: Error: no panel answers: is one plugged in\? \(BUSY stays high after a reset\) 10
reject epaper-sim: panel .*: refresh 2

# hot swap: a new panel, white, gets share 2 (a full reset and init)
epaper plug 2
mark
key 2
expect epaper-sim: panel 2: refresh 1 \(full\) 10
expect epapertest wrote 2 10
epaper shot 02-panel2
expect epapertest longest gap: \d+ ms
# read back with the camera (on the rig, the real one: tests/epaper_hw.txt):
# share 1 is not what was written last, share 2 is
mark
key r
expect epapertest reading back share 2 \(center\)
camera qr MS12NAMEA320ZYXWVUTSRQPNMLKJHGFEDCAXRPP870HKKQRM
expect epapertest read back: share 1, not 2 10
camera qr MS12NAMECACDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ023FTR2GDZMPY6PN
expect epapertest read back share 2: it is on the panel 10
camera scene

# panel 1 again: what it showed, untouched (nothing refreshes unasked)
epaper plug 1
wait 0.5
epaper shot 03-panel1-again
# and written once more (it was asleep: the reset wakes it)
mark
key 1
expect epapertest wrote 1 10
epaper shot 04-panel1-rewritten

# AbortSignal: already aborted, nothing sent
mark
key a
expect epapertest abort -1: AbortError after less than a second 10
# aborted during the reset: the panel is reset, no refresh
mark
key b
expect epaper: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R: aborted before the refresh: (reset|asleep), its picture unchanged 10
expect epapertest abort 5: AbortError after less than a second 10
# aborted during the waveform: it runs to deep sleep, then the promise rejects
mark
key c
expect epaper: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R: abort: the waveform runs, to deep sleep first 10
expect epaper: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R: refresh: done in \d+ ms .*asleep 10
expect epapertest abort 500: AbortError after the refresh 10

# close and reopen, portrait then landscape
mark
key o
expect epapertest: ok closed
expect epapertest open: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R, 128x296, black white red
expect epapertest open: WeAct 2.9" B/W/R, 296x128, black white red

# without the "epaper" permission
launch epapernoperm
expect epapernoperm info: TypeError: ui.epaper.info needs "permissions": \["epaper"\] in manifest.json
expect epapernoperm open: TypeError: ui.epaper.open needs "permissions": \["epaper"\] in manifest.json
expect epapernoperm done
reject epaper:.*(MS12|NAME)
