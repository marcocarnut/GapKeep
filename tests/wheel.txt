# The mouse wheel (src/runtime/wheel.cpp): it scrolls what is under the
# pointer, three lines a notch, as far as that can go, and nothing where
# nothing scrolls. On the Settings page, then the launcher's tiles. On
# QEMU a USB mouse's wheel (patches/circle/0006), one report a notch; the
# rig's mouse has no wheel, so the rig doesn't run this.
expect Keyboard attached 60
expect launcher ready 60
wait 1
# the Settings app: its page is taller than the screen
tile settings
expect settings ready
wait 1
moveto 50% 70%
wheel 2
expect wheel: 1: scrolled \d+ px 10
wait 0.5
shot 01-settings-down
mark
key meta_l
expect launcher ready
wait 1
# the launcher's tiles: a notch is three lines of 16 px text, 57 px
moveto 50% 50%
mark
wheel 1
expect wheel: 1: scrolled 57 px 10
wait 0.5
shot 02-launcher-down
# past the end: it stops there
wheel 20
expect wheel: 1: nothing to scroll there 10
wait 0.5
shot 03-launcher-end
mark
wheel -30
expect wheel: -1: scrolled -\d+ px 10
expect wheel: -1: nothing to scroll there 10
wait 0.5
shot 04-launcher-top
